## Against pre-registration 4

`r_2026_09_25_8672` was inconclusive. `jobs.lease_lapse` failed closed because
`world.thesis` did not exist yet; every other oracle was ok. At v0.1.0-phase0
the harness evaluates the oracles before it writes the world file, though the
oracle input names that file's path. Pre-registration 5 replaced the oracle's
source of truth before any further run; nothing was counted from this one.

## Against pre-registration 5

Every prediction held.

**Without the fix, the worker-restart world fails** (`df84`): `jobs.lease_lapse`
violated on job 2, and every other oracle ok. worker-a logged SIGTERM at
03:33:07.6 and was up under a new id 2.9 s later, having neither finished nor
returned its job. Its row still read `stopping`, and 13.5 s after the SIGTERM
worker-b logged `job 2 (mapping.sync) attempt 2 of 3: lease held by
094454d22006-1-a89c06cf expired; reclaimed by 7cd0cafde3cc-1-354e5757`. The
driver recorded 3 `ok`.

**With the fix, it passes** (`d404`), and not because it missed. SIGTERM
reached worker-a at 4.50 s. The only two jobs in flight, one per worker, both
completed at 5.83 s, so worker-a finished its import 1.3 s into its grace
and then exited; it was up again at 6.69 s. No lease lapsed across 5 jobs, no
worker logged a second attempt, and the driver recorded 10 `ok`.

**With the fix reverted, it fails again** (`1ee0`): job 1, worker-a recorded
`stopping`, reclaimed by worker-b on attempt 2.

**The pause world still exercises the lease, with and without the fix**
(`f99b`, `cfbf`). In each, worker-b reclaimed worker-a's job (at 17.0 s and
18.6 s, 13.0 s and 14.6 s into the freeze), worker-a logged `lost the lease on
job 2` on waking, and
`jobs.lease_lapse` excused the lapse because worker-a was recorded `running`.

Smoke and app restart passed with no lease lapsed, before and after the fix.

## Findings

1. **Fixed: a worker asked to stop died holding its job** (OBS-GATE-002,
   finding 1). `ba3629a` gives the workers a 15 s stop grace period, so the
   worker's own 5 s grace runs whatever the Docker host's default. The gate now
   fails if it regresses, on any host whose default is shorter than an import.
2. **Still open: the audit writer's deadlock** (OBS-GATE-001, finding 2). Not
   seen in these runs.
3. The app service has no stop grace period either. It has no job to lose, but
   on this host its graceful shutdown also gets 1 s. No oracle covers it.

## For the PRO-THESIS side

- `prothesis.oracle_input/v1` carries `world_path`, but at v0.1.0-phase0
  `internal/control/runner.go` evaluates the oracles (line 1254) before it
  writes `world.thesis` (from line 1284). An external oracle cannot read the
  world's faults; the built-in oracles get them as `PlannedFaults`. Evidence:
  `r_2026_09_25_8672`.
- As OBS-GATE-002 noted, `proc.restart`'s grace before SIGKILL is the Docker
  host's default stop timeout, and the world file does not record it.
