## What happened

- **The verdict was INCONCLUSIVE (exit 2), and the Gate job failed on it.**
  The world's `result.json` gives the outcome `harness_error`.
- **Every oracle was ok, the built-in ones and all six of Ontos's.**
  `api.read_your_writes` judged 117 reads, none stale. `sync_jobs.settle` judged
  an import (evidence: [`result.json`](2026-09-28-OBS-GATE-011/evidence/r_2026_09_28_0bd4/result.json)).
- **The harness could not inspect a node after HEAL.** Its log
  ([`harness.txt`](2026-09-28-OBS-GATE-011/harness.txt)):

  > HEAL: perturber: residual fault state survived HEAL: 1 residue(s): netns on
  > engine (unattributed): could not be inspected, so no residual check ran:
  > faults: snapshot output is truncated (last section "", expected to end at #END)

  - The node was `engine`, the app's semantic engine. The fault was on `app`.
    Nothing was done to `engine` in this world.
  - The snapshot came back empty (last section `""`). The engine's own log
    shows it listening, and nothing after that.
  - Treating what it could not inspect as a residue is the fail-safe choice: an
    uninspected node might still hold a fault. So nothing about Ontos was
    proven in this world, either way.

## For the PRO-THESIS maintainers

- **What to look at:** why the snapshot of a node's network namespace can
  come back empty. The node's image (`ghcr.io/fabio-rovai/open-ontologies:1.3.0`)
  is distroless and has no shell, as `prothesis.yaml` notes for
  `resource_return_to_baseline`. No other world of the 325 ended in a harness
  error.
- **Worth deciding:** whether an inspection that failed to run should be told
  apart from residue found. Here nothing had touched the node. A retry of the
  inspection, or a separate outcome ("not inspected"), would keep one failed
  inspection from deciding a verdict.
- The world file and `verdict.json` are in
  [`evidence/r_2026_09_28_0bd4/`](2026-09-28-OBS-GATE-011/evidence/r_2026_09_28_0bd4/).
